## Key scientific caveat (do not overclaim)
Clean extraction is perfect for deterministic methods, but robustness is effectively zero for all
methods under the current sparse additive formulation even at mild AWGN 40 dB (payload success
0%). This is a fundamental limitation of embedding tiny deltas in individual STFT coefficients,
NOT a fixable code issue. Audio quality is reported as SNR only; no MUSHRA/PESQ was run.
